§3 · What the Field Teaches

Scars and Lessons

Thirteen years of drilling decisions produce a specific kind of knowledge. Not textbook knowledge. Scar knowledge — the kind that stays because something went wrong, or almost went wrong, or went right for the wrong reasons.

LESSON 01
Label your uncertainty

The most dangerous person in a room is one who does not know what they do not know. Label it: observed, derived, interpreted, speculative. Make the epistemic status visible before anyone acts on it.

LESSON 02
The map is not the territory

A seismic section is not rock. A model is not reality. Every geoscientist knows this intellectually; the field teaches it viscerally when a well drills into something completely unlike what you predicted.

LESSON 03
Reversibility first

In well planning you exhaust every reversible option before the irreversible one. You can re-log, re-test, re-interpret. You cannot un-drill. This principle applies everywhere that consequences outlast decisions.

LESSON 04
The human veto is non-negotiable

No algorithm approves a drilling decision. A human does. They can override every recommendation. This is not a limitation — it is the architecture. Intelligence amplifies the human; it does not replace them.
