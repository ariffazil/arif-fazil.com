No AVO class (e.g. IIp, IV) is attached to a rock body. AVO is an interface response, not a lithology or age classifier.

Required per interface: exact interface, top or base, incident direction, polarity convention, wavelet phase, Vp above / below, Vs above / below, RHOB above / below, uncertainty range, interface thickness, measured vs modelled status, tuning risk, anisotropy caveat.

Mechanical permissibility

UNKNOWN: no stress, pore-pressure or fault-property data encoded

Present-day stress is a permissibility constraint only. It never dates a Miocene intrusion.

Required for any conduit-reactivation statement: palaeostress and its uncertainty, effective stress, pore or magma pressure, fault orientation, mechanical properties, timing uncertainty.

Seismic relative timing

UNKNOWN: no candidate intrusive feature is encoded with mapped seismic geometry

Relationship rules and implied inequalities are strictly enforced before any magmatic event is promoted.

Biostratigraphic discipline

• Long-ranging species are expressed as 'not older than', 'not younger than', 'and/or older' or 'and/or younger'.
• No long-ranging occurrence is turned into a zonal top or absolute age without the raw occurrence chart and assemblage support.
• Reworking, caving, barren samples, abundance and preservation controls are carried where available.
• Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

Encoding status: PARTIAL - the v2 well windows use zone assignments as reported in the penetration chart. Raw occurrence charts, abundance, preservation and barren-sample flags are NOT encoded, so every window is a zone-level constraint, not a species-level age.
QC flags: See well 'excluded' entries and contradiction C12 (Maligan-1 reversal, Rusa Timur-1 truncated label, Imbok-1 datum conflict, Kudat-1 '?Crocker' with NN9a).

Well witness: surface age windows (GTS2012)

Each bar shows the age window allowed by the nannofossil zones directly above and below a surface pick: the surface must be younger than the rock below it and older than the rock above it. Dots are constant-rate interpolations inside a fully bracketed zone (INT tier). Ticks show the three model ages; the shaded band is the joint window of the non-outlier wells.

Surface reconciliation

Surfaces are the stored objects; stages are the intervals between them. Project ages are top-of-stage ages and legacy ages are base-of-stage ages, but they name the same surfaces.
