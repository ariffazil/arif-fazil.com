§2The Water Reality & Asymmetric Extraction

The Water Deficit

Data centre campuses in Johor are projected to consume roughly 4.0% of Peninsular Malaysia's treated municipal water supply by 2028 — in a state that has endured recurring water rationing crises over the past 18 months. The municipal moratorium on new water-intensive cooling approvals in Kulai and Sedenak represents policy friction colliding with physics.

The Value Drain

Hyperscalers are deploying roughly US$660 to US$690 billion globally in 2026 (Reuters citing Bain; BIS Quarterly Review). That capital expenditure is equivalent to eight Petronas annual budgets. Yet the gigawatt-hours and reservoir levels are drawn from Peninsular infrastructure, while software margins and model rents flow abroad.

The Sovereign Trade Asymmetry

Malaysia provides land, subsidized utility access, and freshwater dissipation for server cooling. In exchange, the country receives low-employment server shells and an extreme reliance on imported AI models that are priced in US dollars. Malaysia is bearing the environmental and infrastructure depreciation of the AI build-out without holding the intellectual or architectural sovereignty of the agents running on the racks.

§3The Labour Market — The On-Ramp Disappears First

The Brutal Metric: 697,000 Jobs at Risk

Malaysia's Ministry of Human Resources (KESUMA) workforce exposure study documented approximately 697,000 formal jobs at high risk of agentic displacement — roughly 4.3% of the formal domestic labour force. These roles are overwhelmingly concentrated in junior accounting, entry-level legal document review, customer service, clerical operations, and junior software development.

The danger is not that 697,000 Malaysians lose their jobs overnight. The structural disaster is that the junior rung of the career ladder is eradicated. When companies deploy AI coding agents and automated analysts, they freeze entry-level hiring. Without juniors today, an economy has no senior practitioners in seven years.

What This Means for Enterprises

Companies that celebrate short-term cost savings by firing junior cohorts discover within three years that they cannot evaluate the truth of their agents' outputs. When you eliminate junior workers, you eliminate the cognitive apprentice pool. The organizations that survive 2027 are those that deliberately preserve the human training forge.

What Survives: The Sovereign Witness Layer

Malaysia should not attempt to burn billions copying American frontier LLMs. The strategic defensive play is owning the layers that cannot be commoditized: the authority envelope, the local cultural and linguistic grounding (Nusantara substrate), the immutable receipt chain, and physical ground truth verification.
