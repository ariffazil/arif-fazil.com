METHODOLOGY · TAKRIF BAYANG INSTITUSI

01 · How to Read an Institutional Shadow

Bayang institusi bukan tuduhan moral, bukan fitnah peribadi, dan bukan gosip korporat. Ia adalah analisis keupayaan, pergantungan, dan hubungan kuasa yang dibuat kurang kelihatan oleh naratif rasmi.

Apa Dia Bayang (Shadow)

  • Keupayaan yang disembunyikan atau dikekang atas motif liabiliti korporat.
  • Pergantungan modal dan superkomputer yang membataskan janji keselamatan.
  • Percanggahan antara piagam kebajikan (charter) dan insentif monetisasi venture capital.
  • Kesan luaran yang dipindahkan kepada masyarakat: beban grid elektrik, air, dan hak cipta.

Apa Yang BUKAN Bayang

  • Personaliti tersembunyi atau diagnosis psikologi pengasas.
  • Bukti bahawa seorang CEO itu orang jahat atau orang baik secara moral.
  • Dakwaan bahawa model mempunyai kesedaran (sentience) atau roh pengasasnya.
  • Spekulasi berdasarkan gaya bahasa (tone) tanpa bukti dasar latihan bertulis.

POSTUR AKSES · WEIGHT AVAILABILITY & GATEKEEPING

05 · Open vs. Closed vs. Hosted

How access architecture determines censorship vulnerability and sovereign control. Open weights guarantee developer execution independence, while hosted APIs enforce central surveillance and real-time intervention.

Hosted Proprietary (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI)

The lab controls inference runtime, logging, and post-generation filtering. Model checkpoints can be silently updated, throttled, or revoked. Users possess zero data residency guarantees unless negotiated under costly enterprise private agreements.

Open-Weights Sovereign (Meta Llama, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen)

Model weights are downloaded locally and run air-gapped without corporate telemetry. Safety guardrails can be modified or removed by downstream users. However, pre-training datasets remain proprietary, and training clusters remain centralized.
