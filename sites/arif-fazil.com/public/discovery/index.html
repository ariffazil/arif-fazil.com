Why these nine rooms

Most websites publish information. This one keeps an institution.

One system, seven organs: arifOS (kernel), A-FORGE (execution), AAA (coordination), GEOX (ground), WELL (health), WEALTH (capital), arifFlow (metabolism). Two boundary services: HERMES carries meaning across the boundary; CHRON carries time.

GEOX reads the ground. WELL shows whether the person, the machine, and the rules are ready. WEALTH counts the capital.

The ledger writes down what happened, gaps recorded not erased. arifFlow watches the metabolism for drift. Evidence lives at /999/.
