## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative convergence of institutional statecraft, esoteric intelligence, and cybernetic systems architecture. Engineered for high-stakes intellectual command, sovereign computing, and strategic foresight, the aesthetic projects unyielding precision, rigorous intellectual dominance, and systemic permanence.

The visual ethos fuses:
- **Neo-Cybernetic Cartography**: Orthogonal coordinates, micro-telemetry grids, vector crosshairs, and wireframe toroidal motifs derived from sacred geometry and topology.
- **Dark Glassmorphism & High-Contrast Structuralism**: Deep carbon-obsidian substrates paired with hyper-crisp, hairline borders and localized emissive micro-luminescence.
- **Editorial Classicality vs. Machine Telemetry**: An intentional tension between imperial editorial serif typography and mono-spaced cryptographic machine readouts.

The interface must evoke sovereign command, absolute clarity, cryptographic finality, and elevated intellectual provenance. It rejects decorative softness in favor of deliberate, razor-sharp mathematical articulation.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is constructed upon deep, cold carbon-obsidian tones, punctuated by a strict tri-chromatic functional hierarchy. Every hue carries systemic, non-negotiable semantics:

### Surface & Substrates (Obsidian Hierarchy)
- **Canvas Base (`#0A0C10`)**: The deepest sovereign void; used for the root environment and global viewport frames.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#10141D`)**: Primary cards, lateral intelligence rails, and container backplanes.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#161B26`)**: Elevated modals, telemetry overlays, active panels, and floating control nodes.
- **Border Structural Base (`#232B3E`)**: The default hairline boundary for container scaffolding (1px solid).

### Functional Tri-Color Hierarchy
- **Electric Cyan / Cobalt Blue (`#38BDF8` / `#2563EB`)**: **Institution, Synthesis & Governance.** Denotes architectural validity, institutional protocols, verified cryptographic state, active telemetry streams, and structural linkages.
- **Sovereign Crimson (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`)**: **Veto, Anomaly & Sovereign Override.** Denotes defensive thresholds, critical alerts, hard state divergence, immutable veto triggers, and urgent intervention directives.
- **Radiant Amber / Golden Sol (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`)**: **Human Narrative & Strategic Focal Points.** Denotes human-authored doctrine, key analytical inflection points, high-value strategic synthesis, and energetic focal markers.

### Luminance & Text Hierarchy
- **Primary Text (`#F8FAFC`)**: Pure titanium white for maximum optical contrast (WCAG AAA compliance against `#0A0C10`).
- **Muted Text (`#94A3B8`)**: Secondary explanatory text, technical subheads, and inactive controls.
- **Telemetry Grid Lines & Crosshairs (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`)**: Low-opacity mechanical overlays and vector alignment grids.

## Typography

The typographic paradigm enforces a triad between three distinct voices:

1. **Intellectual Sovereignty (`Playfair Display`)**: Used exclusively for high-level display text, philosophical headers, and essayistic titles. It introduces historical authority, intellectual gravitas, and ceremonial weight.
2. **Neutral Precision (`Inter`)**: Used for analytical bodies of text, continuous prose, documentation, and explanatory descriptions. It maintains razor-sharp legibility without drawing attention away from high-level synthesis.
3. **Machine Telemetry & Schematics (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Used for spatial coordinates, cryptographic digests, metadata, status flags, timestamps, system outputs, and tabular indices. All labels and badges are transformed to uppercase with expanded letter-spacing to emulate cryptographic instruments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in an **orthogonal telemetry grid** structured on strict 8px/4px base units. The environment mimics tactical command architecture, utilizing clean structural delineations rather than organic fluid distribution.

### Grid Dynamics & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column architectural grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Auxiliary rails (telemetry indexes, navigation coordinates) occupy fixed 280px or 320px column footprints.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Telemetry sidebars collapse into toggleable contextual utility drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Hierarchical sections stack vertically, with data density preserved through horizontal scroll containers for dense cryptographic readouts.

### Structural Scaffolding
Layout elements sit inside visible or implied coordinate frames. Section dividers should feature subtle orthogonal registration ticks (`+`, `[ ]`, or hairline crossbars) at outer intersecting vertices to evoke cartographic and mathematical drawings.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is conveyed not through traditional drop shadows, but through **tonal stratification, dark glassmorphism, and selective chromatic edge-glows**.

### Stratified Substrates
- **Ground Floor (`#0A0C10`)**: Matte, absorbing baseline; holds subtle background SVG coordinate matrices, fractal linework, and wireframe toroidal vector nodes at 4–8% opacity.
- **Tier 1 Glass Panels (`#10141D` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)**: Enclosed with a 1px solid border (`#232B3E`). Micro-reflection is applied along the top rim via an internal inset border (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Tier 2 Floating Nodes (`#161B26` at 92% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`)**: Enclosed with a 1px solid border (`#334155`).

### Emissive Micro-Accents (Glow Profiles)
Shadows are strictly emissive and tied to state:
- **Institutional Focus (Cyan)**: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`
- **Sovereign Veto (Crimson)**: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3), 0 0 0 1px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.5)`
- **Strategic Focal (Amber)**: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25), 0 0 0 1px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`

## Shapes

The geometric philosophy is strictly **Sharp (0)**. 

All interactive containers, data panels, modal overlays, input boxes, and buttons feature crisp, non-rounded 90-degree corners (`0px` border radius). This architectural choice enforces absolute structural rigidity, technical precision, and an uncompromising machine aesthetic.

Where corner accents are warranted, diagonal 45-degree chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) of 4px to 8px are deployed on primary tactical containers or action buttons to reference cybernetic and aerospace instrumentation. Sacred geometry vectors (such as concentric logarithmic spirals, golden sections, and wireframe toroids) are incorporated purely through precision SVG linework and vector masks, maintaining sharp rectangular framing boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Institutional Button**: Rectangular (0px radius), background `#38BDF8`, text `#0A0C10`, font `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase (`11px`, letter-spacing `0.15em`). On hover, emits a focused cyan rim luminescence (`0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)`).
- **Sovereign Veto Button**: Rectangular, background transparent, border `1px solid #EF4444`, text `#EF4444`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)` with crimson perimeter glow.
- **Secondary Ghost Trigger**: Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #232B3E`, text `#94A3B8`. Hover states elevate text to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#38BDF8`.
- **Corner Accents**: Critical command buttons may incorporate subtle 1px crosshair markers (`+`) at the top-left and bottom-right corners.

### Chips, Status Badges & Telemetry Pills
- Formed as compact orthogonal blocks (0px radius) with `4px 8px` internal padding.
- Built with a subtle colored translucent fill (`rgba(x, y, z, 0.08)`) and matching `1px` high-contrast outline.
- Typography: `telemetry-sm` (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px, uppercase).
- Always accompanied by an active telemetry dot (`4px x 4px` square) that pulses via CSS animation for live system parameters.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- Substrate `#10141D` backed by 1px solid hairline border `#232B3E`.
- Card headers feature an integrated top-edge telemetry strip: small monospace coordinate markers (e.g., `SEC_04 // LOC: 84.192`) aligned flush against the right margin.
- Content sections use generous vertical internal padding (`space-lg` or `space-xl`), retaining sharp separation via `1px solid #161B26` dividers.

### Input Fields & Search Vectors
- Background `#0A0C10`, border `1px solid #232B3E`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#475569`.
- Typography: `JetBrains Mono` 13px.
- Focus state instantly activates an electric cyan outline (`1px solid #38BDF8`) with zero border rounding and an inset scanline glow.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Binary Switches
- Checkboxes: 14px sharp square (`0px` radius), border `1px solid #334155`, filling solid `#38BDF8` with a dark `#0A0C10` checkmark upon activation.
- Radio Nodes: 14px concentric squares (outer square border, inner solid square core upon selection).
- Binary Toggle Switches: Linear track of `36px x 18px` with a rectangular sliding block, transitioning from cold slate `#1E293B` to vibrant `#38BDF8` or `#EF4444` based on sovereign authority level.

### Specialized Sovereign Components
- **Cryptographic Digest Block**: Monospaced terminal container displaying key hashes, signatures, and verification statuses with amber micro-accents.
- **Toroidal / Sacred Geometry Canvas Node**: Vector viewport displaying rotating orthogonal toroidal rings and wireframes to illustrate multi-variable equilibrium models.
- **Veto Banner**: Full-bleed high-contrast banner in `#10141D` framed with `2px solid #EF4444` and crimson perimeter projection, alerting users to irreversible strategic interventions.
