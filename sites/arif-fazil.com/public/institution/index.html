How long does it take to revoke an autonomous agent? No public number yet. Probe planned: lease-TTL expiry plus kill-switch drill on a live agent, receipt published to /999/.

answered 2026-08-03

Can every consequential decision be reconstructed after the fact? Learned the hard way: 49 pages shipped to production returning 404, undetected — until the incident forced a verify gate that now makes the failure class impossible. Full record public in /AGENTS.md.

Q4
by construction

Can an auditor verify the chain of authority? Every governed action carries an actor → session → verdict receipt chain. Verify independently: /999/ snapshots and the published identity hash — clone the repo, compare.

Asked against a running system, not against a slide. One answer comes from a dated public failure record — including the number that does not flatter the system that produced it. Two get measured only when the pilot runs, and the weekly report publishes whatever they are.
