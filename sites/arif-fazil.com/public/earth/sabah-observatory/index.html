Flat-plate Airy isostasy, constant densities. It ignores flexural strength and dynamic (mantle) support — which is exactly the point: a summit that Airy cannot support on the local crust is evidence that something other than crustal thickness is holding it up. Flexural support with Te 8–17 km helps over ~20–35 km wavelengths, not 2.5–5 km deficits.

Registered but not drawn — three hypothesis families
These are registered hypotheses that carry no geometry on this transect. They are listed so the model space is honest, not so the figure looks complete.
  • Subduction rollback / back-arc extension — Hall (2013): slab rollback in the Celebes Sea drives extension; predicts regional extension, not a crustal section.
  • Lithospheric root removal / delamination — predicts a Moho step and magmatic pulse; no along-section geometry published for this line.
  • Mantle convective support / dynamic topography — Roberts et al. (2018): predicts long-wavelength uplift; a mantle signal, invisible at 70 km depth.
What the tiers mean. L0 = public measured data (station coordinates, radiometric ages, published well results). L1 = derived by calculation from L0 (recomputed chainage, inversion Moho). L2 = interpretation. Every polygon in this figure is L2. No polygon is a seismic pick.

Well policy. One well is shown: Tepat-1 — oil and gas discovery, 2018, PETRONAS operator release (T1). Placeholder wells carry no outcome, so they are omitted rather than given one. No well outcome on this page is generated.

Telupid. Plotted at its control point (5.62 °N, 117.15 °E → 567.5 km chainage), east of Mt Kinabalu in the Sandakan Division — not inside the Crocker Range axis. Its age is contested (Mesozoic per Tongkul 1997; Miocene zircons 9.2–10.5 Ma per Tsikouras et al. 2021) and is drawn undated.

Depth ranges. Slab remnant (250–400 km) and the drip are mantle-scale; they are annotated, not drawn to scale. The crustal panel stops at 70 km.

What this is not. Not an Earth twin, not a quantum simulation, not a seismic interpretation, not an area-balanced restoration, not a probability or likelihood ranking, not all possible models. No inversion, geodynamic or thermal simulation is run. The physics table carries published material-property ranges; the geometry does not.
