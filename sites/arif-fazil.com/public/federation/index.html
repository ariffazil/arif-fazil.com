The federated architecture

Four layers, one direction of authority. The human decides, the kernel judges, the working roles do the labour, the instruments measure — and every one of them leaves a receipt.

Layer 0 · Sovereign
ARIF FAZIL The human seat. Final veto — no organ, agent or machine below can overrule it. Everything in this system exists under this one authority. /arif/
Layer 1 · Judgement
arifOS KERNEL Judges before anyone executes. Thirteen constitutional floors, one verdict at a time, and a sealed ledger that cannot be rewritten. /arifos/ · /words/doctrine/ · F1–F13
Layer 2 · The working layer

Propose → Challenge → Judge → Execute → Seal

333-AGI Architect. Proposes the shape, then builds it — code, surfaces, instruments. propose · forge
555-ASI Auditor. Challenges the claim with measurement. Senses what others miss. challenge · verify
888-APEX Judge. Issues the constitutional verdict — SEAL, HOLD, SABAR or VOID. judge · hold
A-FORGE Executor. Runs sealed work: reversible, hash-chained, receipted. Never decides. execute · receipt
Layer 3 · The instruments
GEOX Earth intelligence. Basins, seismic, wells — computes physical evidence, never adjudicates. /earth/ · geox.arif-fazil.com
WEALTH Capital intelligence. NPV, risk, market structure — prices consequences before they happen. wealth.arif-fazil.com
WELL Vitality mirror. Reflects human and machine state without diagnosing either. /human/ · well.arif-fazil.com
AAA Cockpit and gateway. Agent identity, protocol routing, the visible state of the federation. /discovery/ · aaa.arif-fazil.com
arifFlow Metabolism. Ingests receipts, tracks FQ, converts experience into the next usable state. internal instrument
HERMES Edge bridge. Where known earth meets the unknown, and where a human voice is carried intact. edge bridge
FRAME Measurement. Watches for drift between what is declared and what is running. internal instrument
Layer 4 · Territories
Human
People, body, dignity. The substrate — never replaced by the machine layer.
Institution

Earth
Known ground and the void beyond it. Evidence first, interpretation labelled.
