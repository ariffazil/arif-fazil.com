6 · Method & Epistemic Discipline

This CHRON operates under strict arifOS epistemic floors (F1 Amanah, F2 Truth, F11 Auditability):

  • Zero Non-Public Information: Every tracked figure, milestone, or legal fact is strictly grounded in publicly accessible corporate filings, court registries, stock exchange notices, or verified research notes. Nothing comes from non-public or confidential sources.
  • OBS / DER / INT Separation: Observed data (OBS, e.g. statutory filings, court dates) is strictly separated from derived calculations (DER, e.g. $/boe clearance metrics) and analytical interpretations (INT).
  • Correction Preservation: When a prior statement is found to be stale, conflated, or inaccurate, it is preserved verbatim in the Correction Ledger alongside the verified state, reason, and consequence. History is never overwritten.
  • Publication Gates: Updates occur only when an event alters ownership, operatorship, consideration, closing date, legal decision path, or the correctness of an existing public claim.
